    My Story as a Designer

    Since last 4 years I run a Design Atelier named ‘EnVisionAr’ based in Nagpur, my hometown, with its wings reaching Japan and Germany. Here, along with passionate Designers and Artists,

    I Design, Re-Design, Invent and Re-Invent Brands.

     

    In the 80s and 90s, I earned my humble fame working on various intelligent Brands like Hyatt Regency, Holiday Inn, and International Film Festival of India.
