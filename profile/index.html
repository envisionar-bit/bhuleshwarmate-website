Hi, I’mBhuleshwarMate.

Also known as Arun. I’m living my dream life of being an independent designer, dedicated to my lifelong passion for branding and design, and still excited for the opportunities to come.

I taught design at various universities and designed multiple brands over the years. Now I look forward to a fresh beginning: sharing my expertise of the design market with you.

( 01 ) As a designer

What I
design

I bring the most versatile creative talents together to pool expertise and offer a wide range of services: from a complete, comprehensive graphic package to the simple process of print production.

01

Corporate identity

Identification marks and symbols, typography, standardization of formats, consistency in advertising.

02

Graphics & signages

Long-term designs and graphic packages like office stationery and equipment. Signages for office and shopping complexes, factory sites, exhibitions and show windows.

03

Editorial design

Leaflets, circular letters, folders, booklets, brochures, souvenirs and annual reports. Mailing cards, self-mailers, broadsides, catalogues, newsletters and house journals.

04

Hotel graphic / culinary design

Food and beverage collateral like menu cards, beverage lists, room service menus, housekeeping collateral, and sales and PR literature.

05

Packaging design

Packages for solid, liquid, powder and paste products: shape, size, material, colour, capacity, opening device, convenience in use, label and carton design, jackets, for industrial and consumer products.

06

Advertising aids

Indoor and outdoor advertising: notices in visual media, price tags, invitation cards, bags, flags, films, T.V. advertising, posters, show cards, cinema slides, hoardings, neon and shop signs, mobile and point-of-purchase displays, kiosks.

07

Public relations aids

Sales kits, newspaper and periodical advertising, tabloids, matchboxes, seasonal cards, gifts, portfolios, conference packages.

08

Photography

Industrial and product photography, exhibition display.

( 02 ) Philosophy

My passion lies in elevating every space, every experience, and every interaction, leaving an indelible mark of beauty, purpose, and inspiration on the world.
